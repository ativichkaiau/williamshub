# Guyton & Hall reference coverage

The GHP reference subject contains original study notes, mechanism chains,
clinical correlations and authored questions organised by the 85-chapter,
15-unit sequence in the publisher’s
[14th-edition contents](https://www.educate.elsevier.com/book/details/9780323672801).
The publisher’s contents were used to establish chapter order and scope.

As of 15 September 2026: **85 chapters, 207 modules and 626 authored questions**.
The practice bank also incorporates the application’s existing generated and
deterministic questions. Registration supplies the same modules to chapter
reading, individual topic pages, search, flashcards, practice and concept views.

## Numbering and existing links

The original reference sequence omitted the urinary-system overview, chapter 26.
Consequently, filtration was labelled 26 and later topics were one chapter early.
The corrected sequence places filtration at 27, temperature regulation at 74,
endocrinology at 75–84 and sports physiology at 85.

Existing module IDs and authored-question IDs are retained. The 48 previously
published chapter URLs affected by the correction redirect to their new locations
in both `/lecture-set/` and `/practice/lecture/`. Links generated by the subject,
search and practice lists use the corrected numbers. Earlier integration
explanations also display the corrected chapter numbers.

The historical paths are frozen in `content/ghp-redirects.json` and served through
the Next.js redirect configuration, so they work before JavaScript loads.

## Maintaining coverage

`npm run content:verify` runs the GHP coverage guard before every production build.
It checks all 85 chapter numbers, curriculum/unit placement, module registration,
chapter tags, reading/practice grouping, content sections, quiz integrity and
legacy redirects. The general content verifier checks duplicate module IDs,
cross-links, quiz answer references and supported inline markup.

When adding a GHP module, register its import and array entry in `content/index.ts`
and keep its source identical to the corresponding `content/curriculum.ts` entry.
Keep IDs stable when correcting titles or notes. If a corrected chapter title is
changed, retain the historical URL mapping as well.
